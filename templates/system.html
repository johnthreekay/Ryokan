Search Tips

Use the uploader field to filter by a specific release group's Nyaa account. The category filter lets you narrow to English-translated or raw releases. "Trusted only" shows releases from verified Nyaa uploaders.

Try searching for batch releases by including "batch" in your query, e.g. "Jujutsu Kaisen batch 1080p".

Grabbing Releases

Click the Grab button next to any result to send it to your download client. The torrent will be added with your configured scoping label (or category on qBittorrent). Make sure the client is running and the connection is configured in Settings.

{% else if tab == "credits" %}

Where the titles come from

Series identity, episode counts, airing status, titles in three languages, and the relationship graphs that let Ryokan follow a franchise through its sequels all come from AniList.

AniList gets rate-limited or goes down occasionally, and its per-episode titles (sourced from Crunchyroll) are brittle: on multi-season series they usually only surface for season one. Ryokan fills those gaps by calling MyAnimeList through Tenrai, a free community continuation of Jikan's unofficial API. Kitsu is only consulted when MAL also doesn't return titles for a given series.

Where the releases come from

Every search result comes from Nyaa, at least until torznab support ships. Public indexer, no gatekeeping, curated by community tags and the honor system.

The shortlist

SeaDex is a community-maintained list of best-available anime releases, keyed by AniList ID. Ryokan reads it either as a score boost on matching releases, or as an opinionated "pick this group" rule via the Ryokan.SeaDexBestSpecification Custom Format. Without SeaDex there's no shortcut to good release groups, just scoring heuristics and whatever groups the user already trusts.

The mapping problem

Seerr and other *arr-shaped tools hand Ryokan requests keyed by TMDB. AniList doesn't speak TMDB. The bridge is anibridge-mappings, a community-maintained dataset of TMDB ↔ TVDB ↔ AniList IDs that Ryokan caches locally and refreshes daily. MIT-licensed (license text).

Custom formats

On a fresh install, Ryokan ships 28 Custom Formats already baked in. They come from TRaSH Guides, the de-facto quality-profile reference for the whole *arr ecosystem. You can edit them, delete them, or reset to defaults from Settings → Custom Formats. MIT-licensed (license text).

Fonts used

The brand mark, page headers, and series titles are set in Murecho, a variable mincho-adjacent typeface by Positype. Self-hosted under static/fonts/, licensed SIL Open Font License 1.1.
